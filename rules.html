How to Play FTO

Tired of just watching from the sidelines? Think you know who's going to serve up a storm and who's going to crumble under pressure? Now's your chance to prove it. Welcome to Fantasy Tennis Open, where you're the coach. You draft the players, you call the shots, and you climb the leaderboard to become the ultimate fantasy tennis GOAT. Ready? Game, Set, Match!

Step 1: The Pre-Match Huddle (Drafting Your Squad)

Your first serve is to build your dream team for the opening rounds.

  • Join the Action: Head to the lobby and choose a tournament to enter. From daily matches to entire Grand Slams, the choice is yours. Remember, once the tournament starts, the participation will be closed.
  • Build Your Roster: You'll get a fantasy salary cap (e.g., ₹50,00,000) to draft your squad of ATP and WTA stars.
  • Play the Angles: Will you spend big on a top seed like Alcaraz or Sabalenka, or will you find value in a dark horse, a qualifier with a killer backhand ready for an upset? A balanced team is key to surviving the early rounds.

Step 2: The Rally (How Your Players Score Points)

Step 3: Second Serve (The Quarter-Final Reset)

The tournament is heating up, and only the best remain. This is your chance to change your strategy for the final rounds!

[Image of a tennis tournament bracket]
  • When it Happens: After Round 4 is complete and before the Quarter-Finals begin.
  • What You Do: You get to completely reset your team. Your initial squad is wiped clean.
  • The New Challenge: You must draft a new squad, but you can only choose from the players who have made it to the Quarter-Finals. Did your initial picks get knocked out early? No problem. This is your second serve to build a championship-winning team for the home stretch.

Step 5: Championship Point (Winning It All)

As the real-world matches unfold, your fantasy score updates in real-time. Follow along live, watch your players rally, and track your rank on the leaderboard.

The user with the most points at the end of the tournament is crowned the winner. You'll earn bragging rights, and depending on the contest, some amazing prizes!

Coach's Corner: Pro Tips for New Players

  • Check the Surface: A clay court specialist might not be the same beast on Wimbledon's grass. Pay attention to player strengths.
  • Analyse the Draw: Does your player have a smooth path, or are they slated to face a top seed early? Plan for both your initial draft and your Quarter-Final reset.
  • Watch for Injuries: Stay updated with the latest news. An unexpected withdrawal can sink your squad before it even starts.
  • Diversify Your Picks: Don't put all your money on favourites in the early rounds. A surprise upset can bring massive bonus points. Find the hidden gems!
