Account Deletion Instructions

To delete your Fantasy Tennis account and all associated data, please follow the simple steps inside our mobile application.

This process is done securely within the app to verify your identity before any data is removed.

  1. Open the Fantasy Tennis app on your mobile device
  2. Navigate to your More screen by tapping ... icon on tabs
  3. In More Screen, Select My Profile to navigate My profile Screen
  4. In the My Profile Screen, Find and tap on the "Delete Account" button
  5. Confirm your decision in the pop-up prompt.

Once confirmed, your account will be marked for deletion and will no longer be accessible. This action will include your user profile, saved teams, and other associated information.

If you have uninstalled the app, you will need to reinstall it to log in and access the account deletion feature.
